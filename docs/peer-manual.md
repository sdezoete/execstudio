## What this is

A tool that turns a short brief (topic, audience, key facts) into an executive-style, answer-first slide storyline — then exports it as an editable PowerPoint deck. It can also take an existing deck and restructure, simplify, or re-template it.

## Before you start — two things to know

- **This preview uses a real cloud AI model (OpenAI), not the company's internal model.** Don't paste real confidential, financial, or customer data into it. Use realistic-but-fake numbers for testing.
- **There's no login.** Anyone with the link can use it — that's fine for this trial, just don't share the link outside the team.

## How to try it

1. **Open the link above.** You'll land on "Presentation studio" with a form on the left.
2. **Pick a task** — for a first try, use *Generate a new presentation*.
3. **Fill in the brief:**
   - **Presentation topic** — e.g. "Q3 business performance review"
   - **Decision you need from the audience** — be explicit, e.g. "Approve a 3% mid-market price increase"
   - **Key facts and context** (optional but recommended) — paste a few made-up figures or findings. The AI only uses numbers you give it; it won't invent any.
   - **Target audience** — CEO/Board, EVP/Senior, Functional, or Operational. This changes the tone and level of detail.
   - **Deck length** — 1–3, 5–10, or 10–20 slides.
4. **Click "Design my presentation."** In a few seconds you'll get a slide-by-slide storyline: each slide has an action-title headline, a suggested layout/visual, and speaker notes.
5. **Explore the tabs above the storyline:**
   - **Quality check** — flags weak headlines, missing evidence, or anything that needs a real number.
   - **Q&A preparation** — anticipates tough questions your audience might ask, with suggested answers.
   - **Export** — download an editable **.pptx** file, plus an optional one-page executive brief and Q&A appendix.
6. **Try "Improve an existing deck"** if you want to see it restructure or simplify a PowerPoint you already have (upload a `.pptx`).
7. **Try "Slide advisor"** (left sidebar) — describe a message you need to communicate (e.g. "why did turnover increase"), and it recommends the best slide layout for it.

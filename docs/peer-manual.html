What this is

A tool that turns a short brief (topic, audience, key facts) into an executive-style, answer-first slide storyline — then exports it as an editable PowerPoint deck. It can also take an existing deck and restructure, simplify, or re-template it.

How to try it

  1. Open the link above. You'll land on Presentation studio with a form on the left.
  2. Pick a task — for a first try, use Generate a new presentation.
  3. Fill in the brief:
    • Presentation topic — e.g. "Q3 business performance review"
    • Decision you need from the audience — be explicit, e.g. "Approve a 3% mid-market price increase"
    • Key facts and context (optional, but recommended) — paste a few made-up figures or findings. The AI only uses numbers you give it; it won't invent any.
    • Target audience — CEO/Board, EVP/Senior, Functional, or Operational.
    • Deck length — 1–3, 5–10, or 10–20 slides.
  4. Click Design my presentation. In a few seconds you'll get a slide-by-slide storyline: each slide has an action-title headline, a suggested layout/visual, and speaker notes.
  5. Explore the tabs above the storyline:
    • Quality check — flags weak headlines, missing evidence, or anything needing a real number.
    • Q&A preparation — anticipates tough questions, with suggested answers.
    • Export — download an editable .pptx, plus an optional one-page executive brief and Q&A appendix.
  6. Try Improve an existing deck to see it restructure or simplify a PowerPoint you already have.
  7. Try Slide advisor (left sidebar) — describe a message you need to communicate, and it recommends the best slide layout for it.

What to give feedback on

  • Does the storyline actually read as answer-first and executive-ready?
  • Is the recommended slide layout (driver tree, matrix, roadmap, etc.) the right call for your content?
  • How's the exported PowerPoint — does it open cleanly and look professional?
